Hi there, 👋

I'm Kenneth, a Senior CAD Engineer with 5 years of hands-on experience in the automotive industry, currently designing seat and interior trim components — from seat back and cushion pads to plastic covers and metal structural parts, as well as door lining components including lower base, armrest pocket cover, pull pocket, and upper base.

My primary tool is CATIA, where I build accurate, manufacturable 3D models and 2D drawings for resin and metal components — designs that have to survive real tolerance stacks, tooling constraints, and supplier reviews, not just look good on screen.

I also work in Beta CAE (ANSA) for geometry cleanup, midsurface extraction, and meshing to support CAE and simulation teams, and I use SolidWorks for 3D modeling and basic analysis when a project calls for it.

What I bring to a project isn't just CAD skill — it's an eye for manufacturability and clean design data, built from real production experience. If you need a design partner who can take a concept from sketch to production-ready drawing without rework loops, let's talk.
